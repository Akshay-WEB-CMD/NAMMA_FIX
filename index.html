FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common queries regarding repairs, warranty support, and service execution.

What does the ₹299 inspection visit charge cover?

+
The ₹299 visit fee covers our technician's travel and standard diagnostics at your premises. If you agree to carry out the repairs recommended by our technician, this ₹299 fee is entirely waived (deducted from your final bill).

Do you offer any warranty on replaced components?

+
Yes, all new components (RAM, NVMe SSDs, Hard Drives, NVR systems, CCTV camera models) carry original brand manufacturer warranty periods ranging from 1 to 5 years. Namma Fix also provides a dedicated 30-day labor warranty on our workmanship.

How fast can you configure a new custom computer?

+
Once you confirm your custom computer specifications, assembly, hardware burn-in testing, and OS/driver optimization are completed within 24 to 48 hours. We then deliver and setup the tower at your home or office in Bengaluru.

Can I access my security cameras on my phone when outside India?

+
Absolutely. During our CCTV installation and setup, our technician configures secure cloud stream connectivity. As long as your home/office network is active and your smartphone has internet access, you can stream live HD video feeds globally.

What payment methods do you accept?

+
We accept Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, UPI bank transfers, Credit/Debit cards, and Cash. Payments are processed after work completion, and a digital invoice is sent to your email.
